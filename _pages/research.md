<h2>Research Overview</h2>

<p>
My research focuses on theoretical and computational studies of strongly correlated quantum systems. I investigate magnetic ordering, transport, and emergent phases using numerical techniques.
</p>

---

<h2>Research Areas</h2>

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Quantum Magnetism</h3>
<p>Frustrated spin systems, non-collinear order, and emergent magnetic phases.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Kondo Lattice Systems</h3>
<p>Interplay of localized spins and itinerant electrons, magnetic textures, and transport.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Strongly Correlated Systems</h3>
<p>Electron correlations, competing interactions, and phase transitions.</p>
</div>

</div>
