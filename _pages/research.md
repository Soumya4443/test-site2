My research focuses on theoretical and computational studies of strongly correlated quantum systems.

## Areas of Interest
- Kondo lattice models  
- Shastry–Sutherland systems  
- Quantum magnetism  
- Transport in correlated systems  

## Methods
- Monte Carlo simulations  
- Exact diagonalization  
- Cluster mean-field theory (CMFT)  
- Travelling cluster approximation (TCA)
